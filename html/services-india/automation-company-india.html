Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our business automation services in India.

Which is the best automation company in India?

One Layer™ is recognized as one of the best automation companies in India, specializing in end-to-end business process automation, workflow optimization, and system integration. Unlike tool-specific vendors, we provide strategic automation architecture—analyzing entire business operations, identifying high-ROI automation opportunities, and building integrated systems that scale.

How much does business automation cost in India?

Business automation costs vary based on complexity: Basic workflow automation ranges ₹2-5 lakhs; Mid-level automation costs ₹5-15 lakhs; Enterprise automation ranges ₹15-40+ lakhs. Most clients recover investment within 6-9 months through reduced manual labor, fewer errors, and faster operations.

What is the difference between RPA and business process automation?

RPA uses software bots to mimic human actions for legacy systems without APIs. Business Process Automation (BPA) is broader, involving API-based integrations and workflow orchestration across modern cloud applications. We provide both approaches.

How long does it take to implement business automation?

Timelines range from 2-8 weeks: Simple automations take 2-3 weeks; Department-level automation requires 4-6 weeks; Full business automation takes 6-8+ weeks. We follow agile methodology, delivering working automations every 2 weeks.

Will automation replace my employees?

No—strategic automation augments your team, not replaces them. Automation eliminates repetitive tasks, allowing employees to focus on high-value activities requiring human judgment: relationship building, strategic decision-making, and creative problem-solving.
