What makes a high-converting video ad?

High-converting video ads share specific characteristics: (1) Hook in First 3 Seconds: Pattern interrupt, curiosity gap, or bold claim stopping the scroll; (2) Clear Value Proposition: Immediate understanding of what's offered and why it matters; (3) Social Proof: Testimonials, statistics, or trust signals reducing purchase anxiety; (4) Strong Call-to-Action: Specific next step with urgency or incentive; (5) Platform Optimization: Format, length, and pacing matching platform user behavior; (6) Sound Strategy: Works with sound off (captions) and sound on; and (7) Mobile-First: Vertical or square formats, large text, close-up shots. We apply these principles through rapid creative testing—producing multiple variants to identify winning formulas.

How much does video advertising cost in India?

Video advertising costs in India include production and media spend: Production costs range ₹25,000-2 lakhs per video depending on complexity—UGC-style simple shoots ₹25-50k, professional production ₹75k-2l, animated explainers ₹50k-1.5l. Media spend varies by platform—Meta ads minimum ₹500-1000/day effective, YouTube ₹1000-2000/day, LinkedIn ₹2000-5000/day. One Layer™ offers production packages: Starter (5 videos/month ₹75k-1.5l), Growth (15 videos/month ₹2-3.5l), and Scale (30+ videos/month custom). Our performance-based approach ensures production investment generates measurable returns through continuous creative testing.

How long should video ads be for different platforms?

Optimal video ad lengths vary by platform and objective: Meta Feed ads perform best at 15-30 seconds, while Reels and Stories work best at 15 seconds. YouTube skippable ads can range 12 seconds to 3 minutes (but front-load value), non-skippable must be 15-20 seconds, and bumper ads are exactly 6 seconds. LinkedIn video ads perform best at 3-30 seconds for sponsored content. The key is matching format to user intent—shorter for awareness and reach, longer for consideration and education. We test multiple lengths to find the sweet spot for your specific audience and offer.
