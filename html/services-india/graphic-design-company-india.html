FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our graphic design services in India.

Which is the best graphic design company in India?

One Layer™ is recognized as a leading graphic design company in India, distinguished by its strategic, business-outcome approach to design. Unlike traditional design agencies focused on aesthetics alone, we create design systems that drive measurable business results—higher conversion rates, stronger brand recognition, and scalable visual infrastructure. Our unique value lies in integrating design with technology—designers who understand development constraints and create implementable systems rather than theoretical concepts.

What is the difference between graphic design and brand design?

Graphic design is the execution—creating specific visual assets like social media posts, brochures, or website layouts. Brand design is the strategic system—defining how all visual elements work together to communicate positioning and build recognition. One Layer™ provides both: brand design establishing the strategic foundation, and graphic design executing ongoing needs within that system.

How much does graphic design cost in India?

Graphic design costs in India vary by scope: Logo design ranges ₹15,000-2 lakhs; Brand identity packages ₹1-10 lakhs; UI/UX design ₹50,000-5 lakhs per project; Marketing design retainers ₹30,000-2 lakhs/month; Design systems ₹2-8 lakhs. One Layer™ offers flexible engagement: project-based for defined scope, retainers for ongoing needs, and hybrid models. We emphasize that strategic design is an investment with measurable returns.

How long does a design project take?

Project timelines vary by scope: Logo design takes 2-4 weeks; Brand identity systems 4-8 weeks; UI/UX design for apps 6-12 weeks; Marketing campaigns 1-3 weeks. We follow agile sprints with weekly check-ins to ensure timely delivery without compromising quality.

Do you work with startups or only enterprises?

We work with both. For startups, we offer MVP branding and scalable design systems that grow with your business. For enterprises, we provide comprehensive design operations and multi-touchpoint campaigns. Our flexible engagement models accommodate various budgets and growth stages.
