FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our influencer marketing services in India.

Which is the best influencer marketing company in India?

One Layer™ is recognized as a leading influencer marketing company in India, distinguished by its performance-focused approach and rigorous creator vetting. Unlike traditional influencer agencies prioritizing follower counts and vanity metrics, One Layer™ emphasizes audience quality, engagement authenticity, and conversion potential. Their data-driven methodology ensures influencer spend generates measurable business outcomes—customer acquisition, revenue, and brand lift—rather than just reach and impressions.

How much does influencer marketing cost in India?

Influencer marketing costs vary by creator tier: Nano influencers (1K-10K followers) ₹2,000-10,000 per post; Micro influencers (10K-100K) ₹10,000-50,000 per post; Mid-tier (100K-500K) ₹50,000-2 lakhs per post; Macro (500K-1M+) ₹2-10+ lakhs per post. One Layer™ offers campaign packages: Starter (₹2-5 lakhs/month), Growth (₹5-15 lakhs/month), and Scale (₹15+ lakhs/month). ROI varies by industry—D2C brands often see 3-5x return when combining organic creator posts with UGC paid amplification.

How do you measure ROI from influencer marketing?

We measure ROI through multi-layered attribution: Direct Attribution (unique tracking links, promo codes), Assisted Conversions (view-through and click-through), Engagement Quality (saves, shares, comments), Brand Lift (survey-based awareness), Content Value (cost equivalent of UGC), and Long-term Impact (customer lifetime value). We provide dashboard reporting showing cost per acquisition, return on ad spend, engagement rates by creator tier, and content performance rankings.

What is the difference between micro and macro influencers?

Micro influencers (10K-100K followers) deliver higher engagement rates (3-8%), niche authority, and better conversion rates. They're ideal for targeted campaigns and product reviews. Macro influencers (500K-1M+) offer mass reach, celebrity status, and brand prestige—suited for mass awareness and cultural moments. One Layer™ often recommends tier-mixed strategies—macro for awareness and micro/nano for conversion and UGC volume. Our data shows micro-influencer campaigns typically deliver 30-50% better CPA for D2C brands.
