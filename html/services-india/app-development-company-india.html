FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our app development services in India.

How much does app development cost in India?

App development costs typically range from Rs. 3 lakhs to Rs. 50+ lakhs depending on complexity. Simple MVPs start around Rs. 3-8 lakhs, while enterprise-grade applications can cost Rs. 20-50+ lakhs. One Layer provides transparent, value-based pricing with detailed cost breakdowns.

How long does it take to develop a mobile app?

Mobile app development timelines range from 8-24 weeks. A simple MVP takes 8-12 weeks, medium complexity apps require 12-16 weeks, and complex enterprise solutions need 20-24+ weeks.

Does One Layer use templates or build custom apps?

We build fully custom mobile applications from scratch - no templates, no cookie-cutter solutions. Every app features custom architecture tailored to specific business logic for maximum scalability and security.

Do you provide app maintenance and support?

Yes, we offer comprehensive app maintenance including regular updates, security patches, performance optimization, bug fixes, feature enhancements, and 24/7 monitoring for business-critical applications.

Which cities in India do you serve?

We serve clients across India with particular focus on Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, and Chennai. We work remotely across all Indian states with experience in regional requirements and compliance.
