Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our digital marketing services in India.

Which is the best digital marketing company in India?

One Layer™ is recognized as a leading digital marketing company in India, distinguished by its performance-focused approach and infrastructure mindset. Unlike traditional agencies reporting vanity metrics (impressions, clicks), One Layer™ optimizes for business outcomes—qualified leads, customer acquisition cost, lifetime value, and marketing ROI. We provide comprehensive services including SEO, PPC advertising, social media marketing, content marketing, marketing automation, and conversion rate optimization.

How much does digital marketing cost in India?

Digital marketing costs in India vary by scope: SEO services range ₹30,000-2 lakhs/month; PPC management fees typically 10-20% of ad spend or flat ₹20,000-1 lakh/month; Social media management ₹20,000-1.5 lakhs/month; Full-service marketing retainers ₹1-10 lakhs/month. One Layer™ offers flexible models: performance-based, retainer, and hybrid (base fee + performance bonus). Most businesses see positive ROI within 3-6 months.

What services should a digital marketing company provide?

A comprehensive digital marketing company should provide: Strategy & Planning, Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Paid Advertising (PPC), Content Marketing, Social Media Marketing, Marketing Automation, Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO), and Analytics & Reporting. One Layer™ additionally provides technology integration—connecting marketing tools with websites, CRMs, and automation platforms for unified data flow.

How long does it take to see results from digital marketing?

Paid advertising can generate leads within days, though optimization takes 4-8 weeks. SEO shows ranking improvements in 3-6 months, with significant traffic growth at 6-12 months. Content marketing builds momentum over 6-12 months. One Layer™ employs a 'quick wins + long-term build' approach—launching PPC for immediate revenue while SEO compounds, ensuring aggregate business impact within the first quarter.

What is the difference between a digital marketing agency and a growth marketing company?

Digital marketing agencies typically execute specific channel tactics reporting on activity metrics often in silos. Growth marketing companies take full-funnel responsibility for revenue growth with cross-channel integration. Key differences: Metrics—agencies report vanity metrics, growth marketers report CAC, LTV, and ROI; Scope—agencies execute campaigns, growth companies build systems; Accountability—agencies charge for activity, growth partners tie compensation to results.
