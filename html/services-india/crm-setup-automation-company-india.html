Which is the best CRM setup and automation company in India?

What is the difference between CRM setup and CRM automation?

CRM setup is the foundational implementation—installing software, configuring fields, setting up pipelines, and migrating data. CRM automation adds the intelligence—workflows that trigger actions automatically without human intervention. One Layer™ provides both: technical setup ensuring solid foundation, and sophisticated automation maximizing efficiency.
