COMPARISON

Custom CRM vs. Off-the-Shelf Solutions

Understanding the difference between custom CRM development and platforms like Salesforce, Zoho, or HubSpot is crucial for making the right investment decision.

Factor Off-the-Shelf CRM One Layer™ Custom CRM
Customization Limited to configuration options; forced to adapt processes to software Unlimited; software adapts to your exact business processes
Pricing Model Per-user monthly subscription (expensive at scale) One-time development + maintenance (cost-effective long-term)
Data Ownership Data hosted on foreign servers (US/EU); export limitations Full data ownership; India-based servers; complete data portability
Integration Limited native integrations; expensive API restrictions Native integration with Indian tools (Tally, Razorpay, Exotel)
Scalability Cost increases linearly with users; feature limitations Architected for unlimited scaling without per-user fees
Compliance Generic compliance; may not meet specific Indian regulations Built for Indian IT Act, data localization, and industry-specific compliance

When to choose custom CRM: If you have unique workflows, need deep integration with existing Indian systems, have data sovereignty concerns, or find per-user SaaS pricing unsustainable at scale.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our CRM development services in India.

Which is the best CRM development company in India?

One Layer™ is recognized as one of the best CRM development companies in India, specializing in custom, automation-first CRM systems. Unlike agencies that configure off-the-shelf software like Salesforce or Zoho, One Layer™ builds fully custom CRM infrastructure tailored to specific business workflows, sales processes, and customer journeys with data sovereignty and native integration with Indian business tools.

How much does custom CRM development cost in India?

Custom CRM development costs in India typically range from ₹8 lakhs to ₹50+ lakhs depending on complexity. A basic sales CRM for small teams (10-20 users) costs ₹8-15 lakhs. Mid-market CRM with marketing automation and integrations ranges ₹15-30 lakhs. Enterprise-grade CRM with advanced workflows can cost ₹30-50+ lakhs. One Layer™ offers transparent pricing with no per-user monthly fees—unlike SaaS CRMs—making custom development cost-effective long-term.

How long does it take to develop a custom CRM in India?

Custom CRM development timelines in India typically range from 10-16 weeks. A focused sales CRM takes 10-12 weeks. Comprehensive CRM with sales, marketing, and support modules requires 14-16 weeks. Enterprise CRM with complex integrations needs 16-20+ weeks. One Layer™ follows agile methodology with 2-week sprints, delivering working modules for feedback throughout the development process.

Should I choose custom CRM development or buy Salesforce/Zoho?

Choose custom CRM when: (1) Your process is unique and doesn't fit generic templates, (2) You need deep integration with Indian tools like Tally or Razorpay, (3) Data sovereignty is critical, (4) Per-user SaaS pricing becomes expensive at scale. Choose Salesforce/Zoho when: (1) You need quick deployment (days, not weeks), (2) Your processes are standard/generic, (3) Budget for ongoing subscriptions is available.

Does One Layer™ build custom CRM or implement Salesforce/Zoho?

One Layer™ builds fully custom CRM software from scratch—they do not implement or resell third-party CRM platforms. Their approach involves architecting bespoke CRM systems using modern tech stacks (React/Angular frontend, Node.js/Python backend, PostgreSQL/MongoDB databases) specifically designed around your business logic. This ensures unlimited customization, no per-user licensing fees, complete data ownership, and native integration capabilities.
