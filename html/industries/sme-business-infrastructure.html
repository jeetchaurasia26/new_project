What Is SME Business Infrastructure? Leveling the Playing Field for Indian SMEs

SME business infrastructure is purpose-built, affordable technology systems designed for the unique needs of small and medium enterprises—businesses too large for basic tools like Excel and WhatsApp, but not yet ready for complex, expensive enterprise software. Unlike enterprise solutions that require dedicated IT teams and crores in investment, SME infrastructure is cloud-based, pay-as-you-grow, and designed for quick implementation without technical expertise.

At One Layer™, we believe Indian SMEs (63 million strong, contributing 30% to GDP) deserve the same technological advantages as large corporations. Our solutions start from ₹10,000/month and scale with your growth—no massive upfront investment, no complex implementations, just tools that work.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about SME business infrastructure

What's the minimum budget for SME business infrastructure?

Starter packages:

  • Essential (₹10,000/month): Accounting + basic CRM + email
  • Growth (₹25,000/month): Integrated ERP + CRM + HR + website
  • Scale (₹50,000/month): Advanced automation, BI, custom workflows
  • Implementation: ₹50,000-2,00,000 one-time

We design phased rollouts to match cash flow.

Do I need to replace my Tally?

No, we integrate and enhance: Keep Tally for accounting it's familiar with, add CRM for customer management, integrate inventory if needed, connect via APIs for data flow, and gradually migrate when ready. Most SMEs use Tally + Zoho CRM combination successfully.

How is this different from buying software directly?

Our value-add: Right tool selection (avoiding costly mistakes), integration between tools (they talk to each other), customization for your workflow, data migration from old systems, team training, ongoing support, and strategic advice. We prevent the "bought but not used" syndrome common with DIY software adoption.

Can you work with our limited IT knowledge?

Designed for non-tech teams: Cloud-based (no servers), mobile-friendly interfaces, training in Hindi/English/regional languages, ongoing helpdesk support, and simple SOPs. We handle technical complexity—you use simple dashboards. Average team proficiency needed: basic computer and smartphone usage.

What if my business doubles in size?

Built to scale: User licenses can be added anytime, upgrade paths to higher tiers, additional modules activated, infrastructure auto-scales (cloud), and same tools support 10 to 500+ employees. We've helped SMEs grow 10x without changing platforms.

Is my data secure on cloud platforms?

Enterprise-grade security: ISO 27001 certified providers, data encryption, automatic backups, access controls, and data residency in India. Often more secure than local computers or servers which lack proper security. We also provide data backup and recovery services.

How long until we see ROI?

Typical ROI timeline: Month 1-2: Efficiency gains, time savings, Month 3-4: Reduced errors, better compliance, Month 6+: Sales growth, cost savings visible. Most SMEs achieve full ROI within 6-12 months. We track metrics to demonstrate value.

Do you offer financing or EMI options?

Flexible payment: Monthly SaaS subscriptions (no large upfront), quarterly payment options, pay-as-you-grow module activation, and tie-ups with NBFCs for technology loans. We also help identify government subsidies (MSME digital grants) where applicable.
