What are agriculture tech solutions and how do they help Indian farmers?

Agriculture tech solutions (AgriTech) are digital technologies designed to improve farming efficiency, profitability, and sustainability. For Indian farmers, these solutions help through farm management, crop advisory, market access, financial inclusion, precision agriculture, and supply chain integration. How much does farm management software cost in India?

What is Farmer Producer Organization (FPO) software and why is it important?

FPO software is specialized technology platform for Farmer Producer Organizations—collectives of farmers that aggregate produce for better market access. Key features include member management, collective procurement, group selling, financial management, compliance reporting, and extension services. One Layer™ FPO platforms have helped organizations increase member retention by 40%, improve price realization by 15-25%, and reduce administrative costs by 60%.
