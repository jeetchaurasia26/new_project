What Are EdTech Solutions? The Infrastructure Behind India's Education Revolution

EdTech solutions are integrated digital platforms that transform how educational content is delivered, managed, and consumed. Unlike simple video conferencing or basic websites, comprehensive EdTech infrastructure encompasses Learning Management Systems (LMS), virtual classrooms, student information systems, automated assessments, and AI-driven personalization engines.

At One Layer™, we engineer education technology infrastructure for the Indian context—handling low bandwidth, multiple regional languages, diverse curricula (CBSE, ICSE, State Boards, NIOS), and the unique challenges of synchronous and asynchronous learning.

07
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about EdTech solutions and implementation for Indian educational institutions.

What are EdTech solutions and how do they help educational institutions?

EdTech solutions are technology platforms that digitize education delivery and management. They help institutions scale beyond physical limitations, automate administrative tasks, provide data-driven insights on student performance, enable remote learning, and create personalized learning experiences that improve outcomes.

How much does it cost to develop a custom EdTech platform in India?

Pricing depends on features and scale:

  • Basic LMS (Schools): ₹1.5L - ₹4L
  • Coaching Institute Platform: ₹3L - ₹8L
  • University ERP: ₹8L - ₹20L
  • Custom EdTech Startup MVP: ₹5L - ₹15L
  • Enterprise Platform: ₹20L+
How long does it take to implement an EdTech solution?

Implementation timelines: Moodle customization: 2-3 weeks, Custom LMS development: 8-12 weeks, Complete SIS + LMS: 12-16 weeks, AI features addition: +4-6 weeks. We also offer phased rollouts to minimize disruption.

Can you integrate with existing school management software?

Yes, we specialize in integration with existing systems including Tally for accounting, existing biometric devices, library management software, and transport tracking systems. Our APIs ensure smooth data flow between old and new infrastructure.

How do you handle low internet connectivity in rural areas?

Our platforms feature offline-first architecture—content downloads for offline viewing, low-bandwidth video compression (100kbps streaming), SMS-based notifications for critical updates, and lightweight Progressive Web Apps that work on entry-level smartphones.

Is student data secure on your platforms?

Absolutely. We implement end-to-end encryption, FERPA/GDPR compliance, role-based access control, regular security audits, and data residency in Indian servers (AWS Mumbai/Hyderabad) to comply with data protection regulations.
