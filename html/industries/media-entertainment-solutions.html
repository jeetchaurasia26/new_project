What Are Media & Entertainment Solutions? The Technology Behind India's Content Revolution

Media and entertainment technology solutions are specialized digital platforms designed for the unique workflows of content businesses—managing high-resolution video files, complex metadata, multi-format distribution, rights and licensing, and audience engagement at massive scale. Unlike generic business software, media tech handles petabytes of storage, real-time transcoding, geo-blocking, and monetization across subscription, advertising, and transactional models.

At One Layer™, we build media infrastructure that powers India's booming entertainment industry—supporting 4K/8K workflows, regional language content, live streaming at cricket-match scale, and AI-powered personalization that keeps viewers engaged.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about media technology and OTT platform development

What is the cost to develop an OTT app like Netflix in India?

OTT app development costs:

  • Basic (Web + Mobile): ₹25-50 lakhs
  • Standard (+ Smart TV, Recommendation): ₹75 lakhs-1.5 crores
  • Premium (+ Live, 4K, Offline): ₹2-5 crores
  • Enterprise (Global, Multi-tenant): ₹5-10 crores

Plus monthly infrastructure: ₹2-10 lakhs depending on viewership.

How do you protect content from piracy?

Multi-layer protection: DRM encryption (Widevine, FairPlay, PlayReady), forensic watermarking (visible and invisible), geo-blocking and IP restrictions, screen capture prevention, tokenized streaming URLs, and regular security audits. For premium content, we implement additional studio-grade protection.

Can you handle live streaming for millions of concurrent users?

Yes, auto-scaling architecture: Multi-CDN strategy (CloudFront + Akamai), origin shield for cache optimization, adaptive bitrate for varying connections, edge computing for low latency, and pre-scaling before major events (cricket matches, premieres). Successfully tested for 10M+ concurrent viewers.

How do you manage content in multiple Indian languages?

Comprehensive multi-language support: Audio track management (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, etc.), subtitle and closed caption workflows, dubbing project management, transliteration for search, language-specific artwork and metadata, and regional content recommendation algorithms.

What analytics do you provide for content performance?

Deep audience insights: Content completion rates, drop-off points, rewatch patterns, demographic breakdown, device and location analytics, content affinity mapping, churn prediction, lifetime value by content type, and A/B testing for artwork and descriptions. Real-time dashboards for content teams.

How long does OTT platform development take?

Development timelines: MVP (Basic VOD): 3-4 months, Standard (with apps): 6-8 months, Premium (Live + AI): 8-12 months, Enterprise (Global scale): 12-18 months. Parallel content licensing and marketing preparation recommended.

Do you support AVOD, SVOD, and TVOD models?

All monetization models: SVOD (subscription tiers with different features), AVOD (ad-insertion with frequency capping), TVOD (rental and purchase), hybrid models (freemium), bundle subscriptions, and regional pricing. Integrated with Indian payment gateways (Razorpay, PayU) and international (Stripe, PayPal).

Can existing content libraries be migrated?

Large-scale migration services: Bulk video ingest with transcoding, metadata extraction and enhancement, AI-powered auto-tagging, quality checking at scale, and seamless user subscription migration. We've migrated 50,000+ hours of content for major platforms.
