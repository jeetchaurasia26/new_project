What Are Real Estate Digital Solutions? The Technology Reshaping India's Property Market

Real estate digital solutions (PropTech) are integrated technology platforms that modernize every aspect of property businesses—from lead generation and sales management to construction tracking, customer relationship management, and post-sales property management. Unlike generic CRM or accounting software, real estate tech addresses industry-specific complexities: long sales cycles, milestone-based payments, under-construction inventory management, RERA compliance, and multi-stakeholder coordination (buyers, brokers, banks, regulators).

At One Layer™, we build PropTech infrastructure that helps developers sell faster, brokers manage relationships better, and property managers deliver superior resident experiences—leveraging India's digital adoption in real estate (90% of homebuyers now search online).

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about real estate digital solutions and PropTech implementation

What is the best CRM for real estate developers in India?

Top choices by size: Small developers: Zoho CRM or Sell.Do Starter (₹15,000-50,000/year). Mid-size: LeadSquared Real Estate or Sell.Do Professional (₹1-3 lakhs/year) with inventory management. Large/Enterprise: Salesforce with custom real estate modules (₹5-15 lakhs/year). We recommend based on project count, team size, and integration needs.

How much does a real estate website with virtual tours cost?

Investment ranges: Basic project website: ₹75,000-2 lakhs, With 3D floor plans: ₹2-5 lakhs, With virtual tours: ₹5-15 lakhs (includes Matterport/videography), Enterprise with CRM integration: ₹10-25 lakhs. Annual maintenance: 15-20% of development cost.

Can you integrate with 99acres, MagicBricks, and Housing?

Yes, full portal integration: Real-time inventory sync, automatic lead capture into CRM, unified response management, performance analytics by portal, and optimized spend allocation. We also integrate with Facebook Lead Ads, Google Ads, and your own website for complete lead source visibility.

How do you handle RERA compliance?

Automated RERA features: Project registration document management, quarterly progress report generation, customer communication templates as per RERA, escrow account tracking, and complaint management integration with RERA portals. Ensures transparency and penalty avoidance.

Do you provide solutions for under-construction sales?

Complete under-construction management: Construction milestone tracking, automated customer updates (email/SMS), payment demand generation as per construction progress, delay notification management, and possession date tracking. Customer portal shows real-time project status.

Can brokers access the CRM for their leads?

Multi-level broker portal: Individual broker dashboards, lead assignment by project, inventory visibility (availability, pricing), commission calculation and tracking, payment status view, and document upload for bookings. Maintains data security while enabling transparency.

What about post-sales society management?

Integrated handover to operations: Resident mobile app for society management, maintenance billing automation, amenity booking, complaint management, visitor gate pass, notice board, and society accounting. Creates lifetime customer relationship and referral opportunities.

How long does PropTech implementation take?

Implementation timelines: CRM only: 2-4 weeks, CRM + website: 6-8 weeks, Complete digital transformation: 10-12 weeks. Virtual tours and 3D content may add 2-4 weeks. We align with your project launch timeline.
