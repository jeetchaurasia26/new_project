What Are Hospitality Tech Solutions? The Digital Engine Behind Great Guest Experiences

Hospitality technology solutions are integrated software platforms that manage every aspect of hotel and restaurant operations—from room bookings and guest check-ins to restaurant orders and revenue analytics. Unlike generic business software, hospitality tech must handle real-time inventory (rooms/tables), dynamic pricing, multi-channel distribution (OTA integration), and deliver frictionless guest experiences that drive reviews and repeat bookings.

At One Layer™, we implement and customize hospitality technology stacks that are built for the Indian market—integrating local payment gateways (Razorpay, PayU), GST-compliant invoicing, domestic OTA connections (MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Booking.com India), and WhatsApp-based guest communication that Indian travelers prefer.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is hospitality technology and why is it important?

Hospitality technology encompasses software systems that manage hotel and restaurant operations—bookings, guest services, billing, and analytics. It's crucial for: operational efficiency (automating manual tasks), revenue optimization (dynamic pricing), guest satisfaction (seamless experiences), and competitive advantage (direct bookings vs OTA dependency).

How much does a hotel PMS cost in India?
  • Budget Hotels (10-30 rooms): ₹5,000-15,000/month
  • Mid-Range (50-100 rooms): ₹20,000-40,000/month
  • Upscale (100+ rooms): ₹50,000-1,50,000/month
  • Enterprise Chains: Custom pricing

One-time setup fees: ₹50,000-2,00,000 depending on integrations.

Can you integrate with MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, and Booking.com?

Yes, we provide channel manager integration with all major OTAs: MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Booking.com, Agoda, Expedia, TripAdvisor, and Airbnb. Two-way API ensures real-time inventory sync, automatic reservation import, and rate parity management across all channels.

How do you handle GST for hotels and restaurants?

Our systems automatically apply correct GST slabs: Room rent (12% under ₹7,500, 18% above), Restaurant (5% without AC, 12% with AC/liquor), Banquets (18%). Automatic invoice generation with GST breakdown, e-invoicing compliance, and GSTR-1/3B report generation for filing.

Is cloud PMS secure for my guest data?

Enterprise-grade security: SOC 2 Type II certified data centers, AES-256 encryption, role-based access control, automatic backups, DPDP Act 2023 compliance, and data residency in India. Cloud PMS is often more secure than on-premise servers with better uptime (99.9% SLA).

Can guests check in using their phones?

Yes, we implement mobile check-in solutions: Pre-arrival registration, document upload (ID/ Passport), digital key to smartphone, room selection, and mobile key sharing for group bookings. Reduces front desk queues and enhances guest convenience.

How long does PMS implementation take?

Typical timelines: Small hotel (10-20 rooms): 1-2 weeks, Mid-size (50-100 rooms): 3-4 weeks, Large resort (200+ rooms): 6-8 weeks. We minimize disruption by running parallel systems during transition and providing 24/7 hypercare support.

Do you support restaurant management for hotels?

Full F&B integration: Room service posting to guest folio, restaurant POS with room charge capability, banquet management with event orders, mini-bar automation, and comprehensive F&B cost analysis. Single guest bill covering accommodation and all dining.
