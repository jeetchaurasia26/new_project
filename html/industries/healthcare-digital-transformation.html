What Is Healthcare Automation? The Digital Backbone of Modern Indian Healthcare

Healthcare automation is integrated digital infrastructure designed specifically for hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centers, and healthcare networks. Unlike generic business software, healthcare automation must navigate complex regulatory requirements (NABH, NABL, HIPAA), handle sensitive patient data with utmost security, integrate diverse medical devices, and support life-critical workflows where errors can have serious consequences.

At One Layer™, we build healthcare automation systems that are NABH-compliant from the ground up—ensuring patient data privacy, clinical audit trails, and seamless integration between OPD, IPD, pharmacy, lab, radiology, and billing departments.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is healthcare automation and how does it benefit hospitals?

Healthcare automation digitizes hospital operations—patient registration, appointments, billing, clinical records, and pharmacy. Benefits include: reduced patient waiting times, elimination of duplicate records, faster insurance processing, accurate billing, NABH compliance readiness, data-driven decision making, and improved patient satisfaction scores.

How much does healthcare automation cost for a 100-bed hospital?

For a 100-bed multi-specialty hospital:

  • Basic HMIS (OPD/IPD/Billing): ₹8-12 lakhs
  • With EMR & Lab: ₹15-20 lakhs
  • Complete with PACS & Pharmacy: ₹25-35 lakhs
  • Enterprise (Multi-location): ₹50+ lakhs

Cloud-based SaaS options available at ₹30-50/bed/month.

Is your software NABH compliant?

Yes, our healthcare automation is built with NABH (National Accreditation Board for Hospitals) requirements at the core. This includes: mandatory field validations for accreditation, audit trails for all patient interactions, incident reporting modules, quality indicator dashboards, document control for SOPs, and staff credential tracking.

How do you ensure patient data security?

Multi-layered security: End-to-end AES-256 encryption, role-based access control (doctor sees only their patients), two-factor authentication, automatic session timeouts, audit logs of all data access, air-gapped backup systems, and compliance with DPDP Act 2023 and HIPAA guidelines. Data hosted in India (AWS Mumbai/Hyderabad) only.

Can you integrate with our existing lab machines?

Yes, we support ASTM E1381/LIS01-A2 and HL7 protocols for bi-directional interfacing with all major analyzers: Roche, Abbott, Siemens, Beckman Coulter, Mindray, Erba, and more. Integration includes automatic result capture, delta check alerts, and QC monitoring.

Do you support Ayushman Bharat and insurance cashless?

Full TPA/insurance integration including: Ayushman Bharat PMJAY package billing, pre-authorization requests, cashless claim submission, maternity and corporate package management, and real-time eligibility checks. We maintain updated package rates for all major TPAs.

How long does implementation take for a hospital?

Typical timelines: Small clinic (1-5 doctors): 2-3 weeks, Mid-size hospital (50-100 beds): 8-12 weeks, Large hospital (300+ beds): 16-24 weeks. We follow parallel run approach to ensure zero disruption to patient care during transition.

Do doctors need to change how they work?

Our systems adapt to doctor workflows, not the other way around. Digital prescriptions take 30 seconds after initial setup, voice-to-text for clinical notes, customizable templates by specialty, and mobile apps for reviewing reports anywhere. Most doctors report saving 1-2 hours daily on administrative tasks.
